# Feeding Basics

## Meal frequency

Adult dogs and cats typically do best with two meals a day, spaced roughly 8-12 hours
apart; puppies and kittens usually need three to four smaller meals.

## Portion consistency

Feeding at consistent times and portions each day helps with house-training, digestion,
and makes it easier to notice appetite changes that could signal illness.

## Water access

Fresh water should be available at all times, refreshed at least once daily, alongside
scheduled feeding tasks.
