# Grooming Basics

## Brushing frequency

Short-haired pets typically need brushing once or twice a week; long-haired breeds
often need daily brushing to prevent mats and reduce shedding around the home.

## Nail trims

Nails should be trimmed every 3-4 weeks for most dogs and cats, or whenever they
start clicking audibly on hard floors.

## Bathing

Dogs generally need bathing every 4-6 weeks unless visibly dirty; cats are largely
self-grooming and rarely need baths unless recommended by a veterinarian.
