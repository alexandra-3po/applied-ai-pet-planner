# Cat Enrichment Guidelines

## Play sessions

Cats benefit from two 10-15 minute interactive play sessions per day, ideally using
wand toys that mimic prey movement, rather than long single sessions.

## Litter box maintenance

Litter boxes should be scooped at least once daily. Cats may avoid a dirty box, which
can lead to inappropriate elimination elsewhere in the home.

## Environmental enrichment

Scratching posts, window perches, and rotating toys help reduce boredom-driven behaviors
like excessive meowing or furniture scratching, especially for indoor-only cats.
