# Dog Exercise Guidelines

## Daily walk requirements

Most adult dogs need 30-60 minutes of physical exercise per day, split across one or two walks.
Puppies and senior dogs typically need shorter, more frequent sessions rather than one long walk.

## Breed considerations

High-energy breeds (herding and sporting breeds especially) often need closer to 60-90 minutes
of vigorous exercise, while smaller or brachycephalic breeds may need shorter, lower-intensity
sessions, particularly in hot weather.

## Signs of under-exercise

Destructive chewing, excessive barking, and restlessness are common signs a dog isn't getting
enough physical activity for its energy level.
